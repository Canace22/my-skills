# Rhythm Distribution Examples

> Real examples from active projects showing rhythm patterns.

## Example 1: 沉渊重生 · 卷三 (Chapters 23-34)

| Ch | Title | 主推进 | 主冲突 | 节奏位 | 钩子类型 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 23 | 南下 | 剧情 | 外在 | 钩子 | 新威胁 |
| 24 | 灵矿 | 世界 | 外在 | 压力 | 信息差 |
| 25 | 竞争 | 剧情 | 人际 | 压力 | 对手注意主角 |
| 26 | 青衫 | 剧情+情感 | 内在 | 钩子 | 信息差 |
| 27 | 故人 | 剧情 | 内在 | 压力 | 主角做决定 |
| 28 | 魂玉 | 剧情 | 人际 | 爆点 | 信息差 |

**Pattern observed**: 钩子→压力→压力→钩子→压力→爆点
**Note**: Two pressure chapters in a row (24-25) works because conflict types differ (外在→人际).

**Note**: Multiple pressure chapters (05,07,08,09) work because each has a different conflict type and hook type.

## Lessons Learned

1. **Adjacent pressure chapters are OK** if conflict layers differ (外在 vs 人际 vs 内在)
2. **爆点 chapters cluster toward volume end** — chapters 10-11 are both爆点
3. **钩子 chapters are rare** — only 1-2 per volume, typically at the start
4. **缓冲 chapters are rare in action-heavy volumes** — some volumes have none
5. **Hook type variety matters more than rhythm position variety** — you can have 3 pressure chapters in a row if each ends with a different hook type
